# Schedra AI 📚🤖
### AI-Powered Student Study Planner and Productivity Management System

Schedra AI is an AI-powered student productivity and study management platform designed to help students organise their academic activities, manage their time effectively, and improve their learning experience.

The platform provides tools to create study schedules, manage notes, organise daily tasks, and track study progress in one place.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Students often struggle with time management, maintaining consistent study routines, organising academic notes, and tracking their learning progress.

Schedra AI aims to simplify these challenges by providing a centralised platform for managing academic activities and improving student productivity.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Smart Study Planning:** Organise and manage study schedules.
- **Notes Management:** Create and manage study notes.
- **Task Management:** Create, organise, and track daily tasks.
- **Progress Tracking:** Monitor study activities and productivity.
- **Student Productivity:** Manage academic activities through one platform.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Improve students' time management.
- Encourage consistent study habits.
- Simplify academic task organisation.
- Help students monitor their learning progress.
- Provide a centralised study management solution.
